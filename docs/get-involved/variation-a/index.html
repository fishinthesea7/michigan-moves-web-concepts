Path 01 · Flexible advocacy

Coalition Ambassador

Advocate for physical activity in your community.

Best forOrganizations and individuals who want to stay connected, promote physical activity, and support the Coalition’s mission with a flexible time commitment.

No meetings. No committee responsibilities.Participate in the ways that work for you.

How you can help

  • Share Michigan Moves resources and opportunities with your network.
  • Promote physical activity initiatives in your community.
  • Stay informed about Coalition news, events, and resources.
  • Connect others to opportunities aligned with the Michigan Physical Activity Plan.

Commitment: Low-lift and flexible. No meetings or committee responsibilities.

Benefits

  • Monthly newsletter with resources, events, and opportunities.
  • Invitations to Michigan Moves events and webinars.
  • Digital Ambassador badge for a website, email signature, or social media.
  • Ambassador onboarding toolkit.

Directory visibility is optional. Ambassadors may be listed only when they provide public-directory consent.

Path 02 · Active collaboration

Coalition Member

Help shape the plan and drive it into action.

Best forOrganizations ready to actively collaborate, share expertise, and help implement the Michigan Physical Activity Plan.

Four virtual meetings per year.Plus occasional contribution between meetings.

What you’ll do

  • Serve on a sector committee.
  • Collaborate with leaders across Michigan.
  • Help identify priorities and develop strategies.
  • Contribute to statewide initiatives that improve physical activity.

Commitment: Active participation. Attend four virtual sector meetings per year and contribute occasionally between meetings.

Benefits

  • Help shape sector priorities and Michigan Physical Activity Plan implementation.
  • Network with 100+ members representing 60+ organizations across 10 sectors.
  • Early access to Coalition resources, tools, and events.
  • Coalition Spotlight opportunities.
  • Monthly newsletter.
  • Digital Member badge.
  • Member onboarding toolkit.

Directory visibility is optional. Members may be listed only when they provide public-directory consent.

A separate organizational pathway

Interested in becoming a Partner?

A Partner is a formal organizational relationship established by mutual agreement, with commitments above and beyond Coalition Member participation. It is not a standard selection on the registration form.

How registration works

Three clear steps

Choose your pathway, complete one online registration form, and receive role-specific welcome information.

  1. 1

    Choose your level

    Ambassadors stay connected without required meetings. Members participate on a sector committee through four virtual meetings per year.

  2. 2

    Complete the registration form

    The form will ask about your organization, sector, geography, primary contact, website, and areas of interest.

  3. 3

    Welcome to Michigan Moves

    Receive the welcome information, onboarding materials, updates, and role-specific next steps that apply to your selected path.
