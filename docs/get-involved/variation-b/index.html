A statewide network

One mission, different ways to contribute

Michigan Moves brings organizations and leaders together to implement the Michigan Physical Activity Plan and reduce systemic barriers to movement.

  • Flexible advocacy for organizations and individuals
  • Active sector collaboration for organizations
  • A separate contact pathway for formal Partners

Which pathway fits you?

Select a role to review who it is for, what participation looks like, and what you may receive.

Both pathways are shown below. JavaScript adds the guided role selector, but all essential role information remains available without it.

Coalition Ambassador

Advocate for physical activity in your community.

Flexible · No meetings

Best forOrganizations and individuals who want to stay connected, promote physical activity, and support the Coalition’s mission with a flexible time commitment.

What you’ll do

  • Share Michigan Moves resources and opportunities with your network.
  • Promote physical activity initiatives in your community.
  • Stay informed about Coalition news, events, and resources.
  • Connect others to opportunities aligned with the Michigan Physical Activity Plan.

Benefits

  • Monthly newsletter with resources, events, and opportunities.
  • Invitations to Michigan Moves events and webinars.
  • Digital Ambassador badge for a website, email signature, or social media.
  • Ambassador onboarding toolkit.

Commitment: Low-lift and flexible. No meetings required.

Optional directory listing: An Ambassador profile appears only with public-directory consent.

Coalition Member

Help shape the future of physical activity in Michigan.

Active · Recurring meetings

Best forOrganizations ready to actively collaborate, share expertise, and help implement the Michigan Physical Activity Plan.

What you’ll do

  • Serve on a sector committee.
  • Collaborate with leaders across Michigan.
  • Help identify priorities and develop strategies.
  • Contribute to statewide initiatives that improve physical activity.

Benefits

  • Help shape sector priorities and Michigan Physical Activity Plan implementation.
  • Network with 100+ members representing 60+ organizations across 10 sectors.
  • Early access to Coalition resources, tools, and events.
  • Coalition Spotlight opportunities.
  • Monthly newsletter.
  • Digital Member badge.
  • Member onboarding toolkit.

Commitment: Active participation. Attend recurring virtual sector meetings and contribute occasionally between meetings.

Optional directory listing: A Member profile appears only with public-directory consent.

Formal partnerships begin with a conversation

A Partner is a formal organizational relationship based on mutual agreement. It is not a third option in the standard join form. Interested organizations should contact Michigan Moves directly.
